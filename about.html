Snappo

One app for your daily life, together with your friends, and your data stays yours.

Your data. Your property. Your value.

📱 Open Snappo (free)

You have one app for groceries, one for parcels, one for Secret Santa and one to plan a meeting. They all want an account and your data. Snappo is one free app for your whole daily life: your things, lists, parcels, drives and meetings, and you sort it out together with family and friends. Everything stays locked on your own phone.

🔒 Why Snappo is different

  • 📱
    Your things stay on your phone.
    Locked. Snappo has no account and no copy.
  • 🚫
    We sell nothing of yours.
    No data, no trackers, no profiles of your lists.
  • 🔐
    Sharing is locked.
    What you share can't be read by the Snappo server: the key is only in your link.
  • 🎛️
    You see and decide everything.
    The app shows every connection to the outside, and you turn it off with one tap.

✨ What Snappo does

  • 🙂
    Together with friends
    Add friends with your Snappo-ID and share lists, draws and meetings, locked.
  • 📷
    Devices & receipts
    Warranty, manuals and upkeep in one place.
  • 🛒
    Shopping & wishes
    Keep a list together, shared locked. Scan a barcode to add something.
  • 💶
    Who pays what
    Split receipts fairly with friends or family.
  • 🎁
    Draw lots
    Secret Santa: everyone signs up with a link, with wish lists.
  • 📦
    Track orders
    Forward shop mails; Snappo reads them on your phone.
  • 📅
    Plan a meeting
    Pick a date together, no account.
  • 📸
    Speed cameras & ⛽ fuel
    Speed cameras in NL and BE, and cheap fuel nearby.
  • 🍝
    Recipes
    Paste the text under a video and put the ingredients on your list.

📲 How to start

Open snappo.info on your phone. On an iPhone: tap Share › Add to Home Screen. On Android: menu › Install app. Done!

Snappo is free. No account needed.
