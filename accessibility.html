Snappo

♿ Accessibility

Version of 27 October 2026.

We want everyone to be able to use Snappo. Our goal is WCAG 2.1 level AA. Snappo has not yet been checked by an independent party.

✅ What is there

  • 🔠 Snappo uses your phone's own font and follows your browser's text size; you can zoom in.
  • 🌙 A dark mode follows your phone, and ☰ Menu › 🎨 Look has other styles.
  • 🗣️ Buttons have words next to their picture, and switches are real switches for screen readers (VoiceOver, TalkBack).
  • 🇳🇱🇬🇧 Everything in Dutch and English, in plain words.
  • 🎨 Colours never say something on their own: there is always a word or sign too (like ✅ or ⚠️).

🚧 What isn't good yet

  • 📷 Camera parts (labels, barcodes, ID papers) need good sight; you can always type everything by hand.
  • 👆 Some handy gestures (swipe, hold) don't have a button as an alternative everywhere yet.
  • 🗺️ Maps and outside sites open at other companies, with their own accessibility.

💬 Stuck somewhere?

Mail hello@snappo.info or tap 💬 in the app. Say what you wanted to do and what went wrong; we answer within 2 weeks and fix it as soon as we can.
