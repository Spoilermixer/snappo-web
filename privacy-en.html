🤝 The Snappo promise

  • 📱
    Your things stay on your phone.
    Locked. Snappo has no account and no copy.
  • 🚫
    We sell nothing of yours.
    No data, no trackers, no profiles of your lists.
  • 🔐
    Sharing is locked.
    What you share can't be read by the Snappo server: the key is only in your link.
  • 🎛️
    You see and decide everything.
    In 🔍 What's where? you see every connection to the outside, and turn it off or remove it with one tap.
  • 📣
    We tell you when something changes.
    Every change is in 🎉 What's new, with its date.

Snappo has no account, no ads, no tracking and no cookies. The maker of Snappo can't see what you add. On the Snappo server the maker only sees totals (like how many shared lists there are), never what is in them or whose they are.

🟩 Stays on your phone

  • Everything you add: devices, photos, bills, manuals, places, friends and settings. It is saved in your phone's own storage for this website, locked (encrypted, AES-256). With the Face ID lock on, it only opens with your face.
  • Reading labels, the picture check and the personal info check all run on your phone.
  • 📇 Contacts (App Store / Play app): choosing a garage or repairman from your contacts, or saving one back, happens only between Snappo and your phone's own Contacts app. Nothing goes to a server, and Snappo keeps only the name and number you put on that device.
  • 🪪 ID papers (passport, ID card, driving licence) are read on your phone and never leave it: never to a server, never to an AI, never shared, not with 📲 Move. Only if you say yes do they go along in a backup with a password.
  • Your backup file is made on your phone, and you decide where it goes. Lock it with a password (AES-256): without the password nobody can open it, not even Snappo. Only a backup with a password also holds the key of your 📦 order address. Your AI key is never in it.
  • 📁 Automatic backup to a folder (computer, Chrome or Edge): Snappo writes the locked backup straight into the folder you picked. If that folder is in OneDrive, Google Drive or Dropbox, that service keeps the file, still locked with your password. Snappo itself sends nothing.
  • 📸 Speed cameras: while a drive is on, Snappo uses your location only on the phone to compare it with the camera list inside Snappo. With 🚗 Auto on, Snappo also looks at your speed while it is open, to start a drive above the speed you chose. Your location is never saved or sent. 🚦 Speed limits: only after you tap 📥, the list of road limits is downloaded from Snappo's own website (the same for everyone, nothing about you goes along) and kept on the phone. 🔇 The 🏠 home spots you set yourself (rounded to about 100 m) and 📊 the numbers of your trips (no places) are kept only on this phone, locked like everything. 📒 With the kilometre log on, the counter readings, addresses and notes you type yourself are kept there too; an export only goes where you share it. 📍 With "Snappo fills in the addresses" on, after a drive only its start and end spot go to PDOK (the Dutch government's address list) or OpenStreetMap Nominatim to find the street address; near a 🏠 home spot nothing goes out. 🗺️ The small map while driving is drawn on the phone from Snappo's own lists (🟩). Only if you switch to 🟨 Real map, the map pieces around you come from OpenStreetMap's server during a drive: that server sees your internet address and which pieces, so roughly where you drive.

🟨 🟪 Only when you tap

  • 🟨 🌧️ Rain radar (only after you turn it on): your rough spot, rounded to about 1 km, goes straight from your phone to Open-Meteo (OpenMeteo GmbH, Switzerland) when you open 🌧️ Rain radar, and during a drive at most every 10 minutes if you turned on 🚗 Also while driving. Open-Meteo may keep it with your internet address in its server logs for up to 90 days. Snappo keeps nothing; the forecast stays in memory. If you type your town instead, only that name goes to Open-Meteo's place search; the town you pick stays on your phone. Weather data CC BY 4.0.
  • 🟨 🐢 Jams and accidents while driving (only after you turn it on in 📸 Speed cameras): during a drive Snappo's own server fetches the open traffic messages of the country you are in (NDW in the Netherlands, Autobahn GmbH in Germany, Bison Futé in France, CITA in Luxembourg). Only the country goes out, and in Germany the names of the motorways near you (like A3). Never where you are: your phone picks what is on your way. The server keeps nothing; the messages stay in memory until the drive ends.
  • 🟨 🗞️ Today in the news (📰 tile, after you say yes once): your phone asks the Snappo server for today's headlines. Only your country (NL or BE) goes along, nothing about you. The server fetches the papers' public news feeds and keeps them 15 minutes for everyone. Snappo groups the headlines on your phone, without AI. A paper's own site only opens when you tap a headline.
  • 🟨 ⛽ French fuel prices (⛽ tile, only in or near France, after you say yes once): your rough spot (rounded to about 1 km) goes to the French government's open price list (data.economie.gouv.fr) to get the prices of the pumps around you. Nothing else. In the Netherlands and Belgium nothing goes out: 🧾 receipts are read on your phone.
  • 🟨 🏷️ Barcode (free 📷 camera in the shopping list): the barcode is read on your phone. After you say yes once, only the number goes to Open Food Facts (a free, open product list, France) to get the name. No photo, nothing about you. Without a barcode the photo is read as a list on your phone.
  • 🟨 🚗 Car details (📒 Kilometre log › 🚗 Your car, or a car in 📦 Devices): only when you tap 🔎 next to the license plate, only that plate goes to the RDW (the Dutch vehicle authority's open data, Netherlands) to fill in make, model, year, fuel and fuel use, the APK date and whether there is an open recall. With 🔔 Check APK and recalls on, your phone does that by itself once a month. When Snappo finds a plate on a car photo in 📦 Devices, it asks the RDW for that plate straight away (pause it in 🔍 What's where). Nothing else, nothing kept.
  • 🟨 🙂 Snappo friends' mailbox (👥 My friends): when you connect with a friend, your Snappo puts a small note with your name on the Snappo server, locked with the key only you two have, so your friend sees your name. A shopping list you share with Snappo friends goes the same way as an invite, and so do 🎁 draw sign-ups and lots for Snappo friends. 🆔 Your Snappo-ID (6 letters and numbers) is kept there with only a public key, so a friend who types it can send you a locked question; the server can't read that either, and removes the ID 400 days after you last used it. 📦 Devices you send a friend wait there locked until their phone picks them up (max 14 days). In a shared list your name and Snappo-ID travel inside the locked list, so the others can ask you as a friend. The server can't read the notes and removes them after 30 days. Pause or remove it in 🔒 › 🔍 What's where.
  • 🟪 🛒 Shop name (devices, only with your own AI key): when you tap 🔎, only the brand, model and model number go to the AI (Anthropic), which searches the web for the full shop name. No photo, nothing about you. Without an AI key, 🔎 opens a normal web search.
  • 🟪 📷 AI camera in the shopping list: only the photo you take goes to the AI (Claude, by Anthropic) with your own AI key, after the personal info check on your phone. What the AI reads comes back to your phone only.
  • 💬 Help & ideas: your message goes through the Snappo server (Cloudflare) to hello@snappo.info, with the version and type of phone if you leave that ticked. It comes from Snappo, not from you, so we don't know who sent it, unless you type your mail address for an answer. The server keeps no internet addresses. If mail can't go out yet, the message waits there for max 30 days. We use it only to answer and to make Snappo better. 🏪 Example mail: when Snappo couldn't read an order well, you can tap 📮 Send as an example in 📦. Your phone first takes out mail addresses, links, phone numbers, addresses, names and customer numbers, shows you every word (you can change it), and only sends it the same way when you tick that nothing personal is left.
  • 🍝 Recipes: Snappo reads the text or screenshot you paste on your phone; it never opens TikTok, Instagram or the page itself. 🎵 For a TikTok link you paste, and only after you say yes once, Snappo asks TikTok's public oEmbed for the text under that video: only the link goes to TikTok, or through the Snappo server (Cloudflare) when TikTok doesn't answer your phone; the server keeps nothing. Only with your AI key and when you tap 🤖 Let the AI read it, that text (and the screenshot, after the personal info check) goes to Anthropic.
  • 🤖 Ask AI: the photo or question goes to the AI you pick (Claude, ChatGPT, Copilot). With an AI key it goes straight to Anthropic (Claude). Their privacy rules apply.
  • Search and shop links: only the words (like "Bosch dishwasher") go to that website.
  • Shared shopping list: the list (and who paid what, if you use 💶 Who pays what) is locked on your phone first and kept on a small server (Cloudflare). The server can't read it. With Who pays what goes a random number for "me" (so two people with the same name never mix up); it says nothing about you. A list nobody uses for 400 days is removed.
  • 📦 Orders: if you make an order address, the mails you forward to it go to Snappo's mailbox (Cloudflare). Your mail service sends them there like any mail; the moment they arrive they are locked with a key only your phone has. Your phone picks them up, reads them itself (no AI) and the mailbox removes them, after 30 days at the latest. When it removes a mail, your phone tells the server only how reading went (read, half read or not read) and, for a mail it couldn't read well, the sender's domain (like shop.com), so the maker can teach Snappo new shops. Never who you are or what's in the mail. Snappo opens no pictures or links from the mails. 🌷 Your order address can also get an easy name (like k7m2qx@snappo.contact): the server picks 6 letters and numbers, which say nothing about you. Stop the address any time in 📦 Orders.
  • 📮 mail@snappo.contact: if you link your own mail address, your phone sends only a fingerprint of it (a scrambled code, SHA-256) to Snappo's server, never the address itself. You prove it's yours by mailing a code from it. Mail from a linked address is locked and picked up just like above; the server only looks at who sent it and the subject, never at the text. Mail from an address the server doesn't know is not kept: it gets one friendly automatic reply and is deleted unread. Unlink an address any time in 📦 Orders.
  • 📧 Copy of shop mails (only if you type your own mail address in 📦 Orders): the Snappo server keeps that address with your order mailbox. When a shop mails your Snappo address straight away, the server sends that mail on to your address at once (you see the shop's mail inside the copy), from Snappo's own mailbox at one.com (Snappo's mail provider), and keeps nothing. Mail you sent yourself is not sent back. Remove the address any time in 📦 Orders; stopping your order address removes it too.
  • 📲 Move to a new phone: your phone locks a copy of everything and puts it on Snappo's server (Cloudflare) for max 1 hour. The key is only in the code you show your new phone, never sent. Once the new phone has it, the copy is removed. The AI key and order address don't come along.
  • 🎁 Sign-up link for a draw: if you share one, everyone who taps it can add their name (and, if they want, what they like and a wish). That is locked on their phone with a key that is only in the link, and waits on Snappo's server (Cloudflare) until your phone picks it up. The server can't read it. When you draw, or after 90 days at the latest, it is removed.
  • 📅 Plan a meeting: a poll you share (its name, times, and the place or meeting link if you add them) and every answer (a name and ✅ 🤔 ❌ per time) are locked on the phones with a key that is only in the link, and kept on Snappo's server (Cloudflare). The server can't read them. Who you ask stays on your phone. Removed 90 days after the last change, or when you delete the meeting. 📅 Add to my calendar makes a file on your phone; Google Calendar and Outlook open only when you tap them, with only that meeting.
  • ✏️ Wish cards for a draw: only if the organiser ticks "Everyone can change their wishes after the draw". Each person then gets a small card on Snappo's server (Cloudflare) with their name, wishes and likes, locked with a key that is only in the lot links. Your own link lets you change your wishes; everyone in the draw can read them (their lot link holds the keys). The server can't read them. The organiser empties them by deleting the draw; unused cards are removed after 400 days.
  • WhatsApp and share buttons open those apps with the text you see. A 🎁 lot link holds only that one lot (who gives to whom, the date and budget, and what they like if they said so), and goes only to the person you send it to.

🟨 📊 Anonymous button counts (on by default while Snappo is being tested)

  • To understand and improve our app, your phone counts which Snappo button or tile you tap. Never what you type or see, never when, never a name, number or id.
  • Once a week one package goes to the Snappo server: the counts, your look and your language. The server adds it to the totals of everyone and keeps nothing about the sender, so nobody can see what one person did.
  • 🚧 When you answer 👍 or 👎 on a beta part (like ⛽ Fuel) or on the 💬 chat question, only that part and your thumb go to the server at once, to count the totals. Nothing else.
  • Turn it off with one tap: the first time Snappo tells you about it, or in 🔒 › 🔍 What's where? › 📊. Before Snappo goes into the App Store and Google Play it will be off until you turn it on.

⏸️ You decide

  • 🕵️ Apps on your phone (🛡️ Privacy › Check my apps): you pick your iPhone's own App Privacy Report file and Snappo reads it on your phone 🟩. It is not saved and nothing goes out. Only if you paste an app's privacy statement and tap 🟪 Explain it, that pasted text goes to the AI with your own AI key.
  • 🛡️ Privacy report: from the 1st of every month it waits in 🔔 Attention! for 2 days, and it is always in the 🛡️ Privacy tile and ☰ Menu. It shows what is kept on your phone 🟩, every connection to the outside 🟨 (each shared list, sign-up link and poll on its own, with its own buttons) and the AI 🟪. It is made on your phone. To tell you when something was last used, Snappo keeps the day each connection was last used, only on this phone; it never goes anywhere.
  • In 🔒 › 🔍 What's where? you can pause or remove every connection above, or turn everything off at once. A paused connection sends nothing at all, until you turn it on again.
  • 🗑️ Erase everything (at the bottom of 🔍 What's where?) removes your things from the Snappo server first, then wipes everything from this phone.

🛡️ Security

  • Found a security problem? Mail hello@snappo.info (also in snappo.info/.well-known/security.txt). We fix it first.
  • Linked mail addresses are kept as a fingerprint mixed with a secret (a "pepper") that is not stored next to them. Each internet address may only make a few new things a day, to stop misuse; it is only kept scrambled, for 1 day.
  • If something ever goes wrong at the Snappo server, we tell you in Snappo, and the Dutch privacy authority within 72 hours.

✉️ Questions?

Mail hello@snappo.info. Because Snappo keeps nothing about you, we can't look up or delete your things: they are on your phone, and you delete them there.

🍪 Cookies

Snappo uses no cookies and no trackers. What Snappo keeps on your phone (in your browser storage) is needed for the app to work and is locked.
